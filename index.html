Our Specialty Focus

Big Game Theory is a specialized preservation-focused operation dedicated to select high-standard poultry strains. We intentionally focus our facility on four distinct bloodlines: Blue Rosecomb Bantams, Pure Surinam Black & Blues, Porcelain D'Uccle Bantams, and in the coming future, Aseel Oriental Gamefowl. Through rigorous selection, genetic tracking, and line breeding, we protect historic phenotypes while setting new benchmarks for vigor and type.

Our Bloodline Standards

🌹

BLUE ROSECOMBS

Precision rose combs, snow-white earlobes, and clean slate blue/splash feathering on compact bantam frames.
